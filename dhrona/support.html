Dhrona — Support

Dhrona is a 24-hour accountability tracker. Every minute of your day lands in a category you named, or shows up as unaccounted.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback: kaif.master02@gmail.com

Your account

From version 1.1 Dhrona opens with an account — sign in with Apple or Google. It is what carries your ledger to a new phone. Signing out leaves everything on this iPhone untouched; you sign in again to get back in.

Screen Time access

Dhrona asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) permission to categorize which apps count as wasted time. This is self-monitoring only, and no Screen Time data leaves your device: the apps you pick are held in an opaque system token Dhrona cannot read and never transmits, and per-app usage is never read or reported to anyone.

Your data

Your ledger is stored on your iPhone and synced to your account so it outlives the phone. You can export it to CSV or JSON at any time from Settings — that export is a local file you keep, separate from the backup. Settings → Account → Delete account removes the account and everything synced to it.
