Dhrona — Support

Dhrona is a 24-hour accountability tracker. Everything stays on your iPhone — no account, no servers, no analytics.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback: kaif.master02@gmail.com

Screen Time access

Dhrona asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) permission to categorize which apps count as wasted time. This is self-monitoring only — Dhrona never reads or reports your per-app usage to anyone, and nothing leaves your device.

Data

Dhrona has no network layer. All data is stored locally on your device. You can export it to CSV or JSON at any time from within the app.
