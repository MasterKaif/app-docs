Dhrona — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Dhrona is a 24-hour accountability tracker. It has no account system, no servers, and no network layer of any kind. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

Data we collect

None. Dhrona does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics — because it has no way to send anything anywhere. There is no backend, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into Dhrona — hourly logs, categories, notes — is stored locally on your iPhone using on-device storage (SwiftData) and, where relevant, an on-device App Group shared with the Dhrona widget. Nothing leaves your device. You can export your data to CSV or JSON at any time from within the app, and you control where that export goes.

Screen Time access

Dhrona asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) permission to categorize which apps count as wasted time. This is self-monitoring only: the user authorizes monitoring of their own device, for themselves. Dhrona never reads or reports your per-app usage to anyone — it only reconstructs approximate usage from on-device threshold callbacks and attributes those minutes to a category in your own local ledger.

Children's privacy

Dhrona is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age — see "Data we collect" above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
