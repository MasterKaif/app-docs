Dhrona — Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Some regions (for example, under the CCPA/CPRA) require apps to give users a way to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. This page is that notice for Dhrona.

There is nothing to opt out of

Dhrona does not sell or share your personal information with anyone, and does not disclose it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics, and no third-party trackers in the app. Because there is no sale or sharing in the first place, there is nothing to opt out of.

What we do hold

Dhrona requires an account from version 1.1, and your ledger syncs to it so it survives a replaced phone. That is held on our behalf by our hosting provider and is isolated to your account. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored on your device, what syncs, and why no Screen Time data ever leaves your iPhone.

Deleting everything

Settings → Account → Delete account erases your account and everything synced to it, without needing to contact us.
