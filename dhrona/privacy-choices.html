Dhrona — Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Some regions (for example, under the CCPA/CPRA) require apps to give users a way to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. This page is that notice for Dhrona.

There is nothing to opt out of

Dhrona does not sell, share, or transmit any personal information to anyone. It has no account system, no servers, no third-party SDKs, and no analytics or advertising trackers. All data you enter stays on your device. Because no data is collected or shared in the first place, there is no sale or sharing to opt out of.

See the full Privacy Policy for details on what is stored locally and how Screen Time access is used.
